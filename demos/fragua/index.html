(01) The place

No mirrors to pose in. Just iron, heat and people who come back.

Fragua opened in 2019 inside a foundry that had been cold for thirty years. We kept the walls, the crane and the smell of metal. We swapped the furnace for barbells.

Small groups, coaches who know your name and a plan that changes with you every month. No endless contracts, no fine print.

Twelve hundred metres of fire.
